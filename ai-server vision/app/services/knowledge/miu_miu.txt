=== MIU MIU ===

--- BRAND NOTE ---
Miu Miu is Prada's sister brand — founded by Miuccia Prada
"MIU MIU" embossed on interior
More playful/whimsical aesthetic than Prada
Gold hardware dominates
Do not confuse materials with Prada — Miu Miu uses softer leathers
No Saffiano crosshatch — Miu Miu uses smooth/washed/crystal leathers

--- MATERIALS (identify first) ---
Washed/Nappa Leather: SIGNATURE Miu Miu material
  → deliberately soft, slightly wrinkled appearance
  → matte finish, very supple
  → PHOTO TIP: Looks intentionally relaxed/worn even when new
  → most iconic Miu Miu material

Madras Leather: Colorful patchwork leather
  → multiple color panels stitched together
  → very distinctive playful look

Crystal/Embellished: Crystal embellishments on leather
  → very glamorous, evening aesthetic
  → crystals catch light in photos

Satin: Fabric, high sheen
  → seasonal, evening bags

Velvet: Fabric with nap
  → seasonal, rich texture

Shearling/Fur: Fluffy texture
  → seasonal, very distinctive

Quilted Leather: Diamond or geometric quilt
  → used on specific models

WASHED LEATHER COLOR NOTE:
Washed Nappa shows color with slight unevenness/variation
  → same color bag looks slightly varied in tone = normal
  → part of intentional washed aesthetic
  → color appears slightly muted/vintage compared to smooth leather

COLOR CONFUSION:
Cipria vs Rosa: Cipria is nude-dominant with pink hint.
  Rosa is clearly pink.
Cameo vs Cipria: Cameo is more dusty/vintage rose.
  Cipria is paler/more nude.
Orchidea vs Glicine: Orchidea has pink dominance.
  Glicine is more purple/wisteria.
Malva vs Glicine: Malva is more grey-muted mauve.
  Glicine is cleaner purple.
Avorio vs Bianco: Avorio is warm/cream. Bianco is pure cool white.
Carminio vs Bordeaux: Carminio is brighter/more vivid red.
  Bordeaux is deeper/more purple-wine.
Fumo vs Grigio: Fumo is darker/smokier. Grigio is medium neutral grey.

--- MIU MIU VS PRADA (common confusion) ---
Both same house but different aesthetic:

Material:
  Miu Miu = washed/soft nappa, intentionally relaxed
  Prada = Saffiano crosshatch or structured Nappa

Hardware:
  Miu Miu = "MIU MIU" lettering, playful
  Prada = triangle enamel plaque or rectangular PRADA plate

Aesthetic:
  Miu Miu = whimsical, playful, softer
  Prada = structured, minimal, architectural

Quilt:
  Miu Miu Matelassé = large geometric oversized quilt
  Prada Padded = pillow-soft overall shape, not geometric quilt

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Washed Nappa: Pre-softened = harder to assess wear
  → check color fading at handle attachment + base corners
  → genuine wear shows as color lightening on high contact areas
  → tears/cuts clearly visible against soft surface
Crystal embellishments: Check for missing crystals
  → empty settings visible as small holes/dark spots
  → crystals around edges fall off first
Matelassé quilting: Check for flattening of large quilt squares
  → quilt loses definition with heavy use
Smooth leather: Scratches show as lighter marks
Hardware: Gold MIU MIU lettering wears on raised letters first
  → plating chips on letter edges
Chain strap: Check for darkening/plating loss
Shearling/fur: Check for matting, bald patches, discoloration
  → fur condition very visible in photos
Satin: Check for snags, pulls, staining
  → fabric bags show wear differently than leather
