=== DIOR ===

--- QUILT PATTERNS (identify first) ---
Cannage: Small diamond quilt, tight uniform pattern — SIGNATURE DIOR
  → used on Lady Dior, Miss Dior, 30 Montaigne
Macro Cannage: Same diamond but OVERSIZED/puffed squares, very visible
  → used on Caro only
Embroidered Fabric: Not leather — woven fabric with embroidered designs
  → used on Book Tote only

--- MODELS ---
Lady Dior (XS/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured rectangle, flat bottom, slightly tapered top
QUILT: Cannage (small diamonds) ← #1 identifier
HANDLES: Short top handles with D-I-O-R letter charms hanging ← #2 identifier
STRAP: Removable shoulder/crossbody strap
CLOSURE: Zip top
HARDWARE: CD clasp on front (decorative, not functional closure)
CONFUSED WITH Miss Dior: Lady Dior has DIOR letter charms on handles. Miss Dior does not.
CONFUSED WITH Caro: Lady Dior has small Cannage. Caro has large Macro Cannage.
SIZES: XS=17cm | Small=20cm | Medium=24cm | Large=28cm

Book Tote (Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Large structured open tote, boxy rectangular
MATERIAL: Embroidered fabric exterior ← #1 identifier (NOT leather)
CLOSURE: None — open top
HANDLES: Short fabric top handles
DESIGN: Seasonal embroidery patterns vary enormously
REVERSIBLE VERSION: Two-sided, different design each side
NOTE: Describe embroidery theme (e.g. "blue toile de jouy", "floral multicolor on white")
CONFUSED WITH nothing — embroidered fabric is unique in Dior lineup

30 Montaigne (Box/East-West):
SHAPE: Structured box, clean geometric
CLOSURE: CD CLASP (interlocked C and D) ← #1 identifier
HANDLES: Short top handle + removable strap
HARDWARE: Very prominent CD clasp centered on front
BOX version: Taller, more cube-like
EAST-WEST version: Wider, more horizontal/landscape orientation
CONFUSED WITH Bobby: 30 Montaigne is structured box. Bobby is soft hobo.

Saddle Bag (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: SADDLE/HALF-MOON shape — asymmetric curved bottom ← unmistakable #1 identifier
CLOSURE: Flap with CD buckle strap across front
STRAP: Crossbody strap
HARDWARE: CD logo buckle on front strap
NOTE: Shape is completely unique — cannot be confused once identified
ORIGIN: Original 2000s John Galliano design, relaunched 2018
SIZES: Mini=tiny crossbody | Small=compact | Medium=generous

Bobby (East-West/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Soft hobo, relaxed unstructured, slightly crescent
CLOSURE: CD clasp on flap
STRAP: Shoulder or crossbody
CONFUSED WITH 30 Montaigne: Bobby is soft/slouchy. 30 Montaigne is rigid box.
CONFUSED WITH Caro: Bobby has no quilt pattern. Caro has Macro Cannage.

Caro (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Structured flap, modern clean lines
QUILT: MACRO CANNAGE (large oversized puffed diamonds) ← #1 identifier
CLOSURE: CD clasp
STRAP: Chain or leather
CONFUSED WITH Lady Dior: Caro has large puffed quilt. Lady Dior has small tight quilt + letter charms.

Miss Dior:
SHAPE: Small structured flap
QUILT: Cannage (small diamonds, same as Lady Dior)
CLOSURE: CD turn-lock
STRAP: Chain strap
CONFUSED WITH Lady Dior: Miss Dior has NO letter charms on handles + chain strap only

--- LEATHERS (photo identification) ---
Cannage quilted leather: Small tight diamonds, structured surface
  → most common Lady Dior/Miss Dior material

Macro Cannage: Same diamond shape but large/puffed, almost pillow-like squares
  → Caro exclusive

Ultramatte calfskin: Completely matte smooth surface, zero shine
  → very modern look, used on 30 Montaigne and others
  → PHOTO TIP: Unusually flat/dead matte — no light reflection at all

Smooth Calfskin: Polished smooth, slight natural sheen
  → classic clean look

Embroidered fabric: Woven textile, visible embroidery stitching
  → Book Tote exclusive

--- HARDWARE ---
CD Clasp: Interlocked C and D letters, very recognizable
  → used on 30 Montaigne, Bobby, Caro
CD Buckle: CD logo on leather strap buckle
  → Saddle Bag exclusive
DIOR Letter Charms: D-I-O-R individual letter pendants on handles
  → Lady Dior exclusive, gold or silver
Turn-lock: Rotating lock mechanism
  → Miss Dior

HARDWARE COLORS: Gold (most common), Silver, Aged Gold (vintage pieces)

COLOR CONFUSION:
Midnight vs Noir: Midnight shows blue in light. Noir is flat.
Burgundy vs Cherry: Burgundy deeper/more purple. Cherry brighter/more red.
Blush vs Nude: Blush has clear pink. Nude is beige-dominant.
Sand vs Nude: Sand is more yellow-beige. Nude has pink undertone.
Sage vs Powder Blue: Both muted pastels — Sage has green. Blue has cool blue.

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Cannage quilting: Check diamonds for flattening/distortion with heavy use
Letter charms: Check all 4 charms present (D-I-O-R), check for scratches
Hardware: CD clasp wear — gold plating fades on high-contact points
Corners: Leather lightens/wears at corners first
Embroidered fabric (Book Tote): Check for pulls, fraying, staining on fabric
Strap: Check strap hardware attachment points for wear
