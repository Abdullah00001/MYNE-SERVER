=== FENDI ===

--- BRAND NOTE ---
FF logo (double F interlocked) = primary Fendi identifier
Zucca/Zucchino canvas = signature Fendi pattern
"FENDI ROMA" embossed on interior

--- MATERIALS (identify first) ---
Zucca Canvas: Larger FF logo pattern on coated canvas
  → warm tan/brown tones, FF clearly visible
  → structured, durable

Zucchino Canvas: Smaller FF pattern, same colorway
  → denser/smaller repeat than Zucca

Nappa: Smooth soft calfskin
  → used on Peekaboo, First, By The Way

Selleria: Thick saddle leather with VISIBLE CONTRAST STITCHING
  → PHOTO TIP: Chunky visible stitching around edges
  → very artisanal/handmade look

Patent: High gloss mirror finish

--- MODELS ---
Baguette (Mini/Regular/Large):
SHAPE: LONG NARROW rectangle worn under arm ← #1 identifier
  → proportions like a French baguette bread
  → short strap designed to tuck under arm
CLOSURE: FF logo clasp or decorative closure on flap ← #2 identifier
STRAP: Short shoulder strap
NOTE: Comes in thousands of variations — fabric, leather, beaded, fur
  → identify by shape first, then material/closure
CONFUSED WITH nothing — narrow elongated under-arm shape unique
SIZES: Mini=tiny | Regular=26cm | Large=32cm

Peekaboo (Micro/Mini/Regular/Large/XL):
SHAPE: Structured top handle, rounded bottom
HANDLES: Short rigid top handles
CLOSURE: FF clasp on front, opens to reveal interior ← #1 identifier
  → bag SPLITS OPEN revealing colorful interior lining
  → "peekaboo" = see inside when opened
INTERIOR: Contrasting color lining visible when clasp opened
CONFUSED WITH nothing — split-open reveal is unique
SIZES: Micro=tiny | Mini=small | Regular=medium | Large=generous | XL=large

Sunshine (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Structured tote, CURVED/ROUNDED top edge ← #1 identifier
HANDLES: Short top handles
CLOSURE: Open top or zip
LOGO: FF lettering visible on handles or body
NOTE: Clean structured tote, rounded silhouette distinctive

First (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Structured top handle, POINTED/ANGULAR bottom corners ← #1 identifier
  → bottom corners are sharp/pointed, not rounded
HANDLES: Single top handle, sculptural
CLOSURE: F logo hardware on front
STRAP: Removable shoulder strap
CONFUSED WITH Peekaboo: First has pointed corners + single handle.
  Peekaboo has rounded bottom + double handles + split closure

By The Way (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured crossbody/shoulder, clean rectangle
HANDLES: Short top handle + shoulder strap
CLOSURE: Zip top
NOTE: Clean everyday bag, less distinctive than other Fendi models
  → identified by FF hardware + "FENDI ROMA" embossing

O'Lock (Mini/Continental):
SHAPE: Small structured bag or wallet
CLOSURE: FF O'LOCK HARDWARE ← #1 identifier
  → round circular lock with FF cutout
  → very distinctive circular metal hardware
STRAP: Shoulder strap or chain

Kan I:
SHAPE: Structured rectangular top handle
HANDLES: Short top handles
CLOSURE: FF logo clasp, geometric
HARDWARE: Very prominent FF geometric hardware on front ← #1 identifier

--- HARDWARE ---
FF Clasp (Baguette): Various decorative closures, FF logo common
O'Lock: Round circular FF cutout ← O'Lock exclusive
F Hardware (First): F-shaped closure on front
FF Logo Clasp (Peekaboo): Opens bag fully
FF Lettering: Block letter hardware on various models

HARDWARE COLORS:
Gold/Brass: Most common
Silver: Available
Black: Seasonal/limited

Fabric: Silk, jacquard, tweed
Embellished: Beaded, sequined, embroidered
Fur: Various fur versions
Limited editions: Collaborations (Fendi x Versace etc)

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Zucca canvas: Check corners for peeling/cracking
Selleria leather: Check contrast stitching for loosening/fraying
  → stitching is thick and visible — wear shows clearly
Baguette strap: Short strap gets heavy wear
  → check strap attachment points
Peekaboo clasp: Opening mechanism gets heavy use
  → check for scratching on FF clasp
First pointed corners: Corners are sharp — first to wear
  → leather lightens/scuffs at points
Bamboo details: If present check for cracks/chips
Hardware: Gold plating wears on clasp contact points
Interior (Peekaboo): Colorful lining visible — check for staining
