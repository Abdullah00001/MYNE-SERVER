LUXURY BAG KNOWLEDGE BASE
=========================

=== PHOTO DETECTION RULES ===
- Single front photo: can detect model, leather, color, hardware, size, variant
- Multiple angles needed for: bi-color, tri-color, HSS stitching, stamp letter, verso interior
- Stamp letter: embossed inside bag near base or under flap — only visible from interior photo
- HSS stitching: tiny detail, requires closeup — if not clearly visible assume standard
- Bi-color second panel: usually on back, sides, handles, or trim — may be invisible from front alone
- When uncertain due to single/poor photo: note in analysis field, do not guess special classifications

BI-COLOR DETECTION RULES:
- Bi-color does NOT require high contrast. Even similar shades count (e.g. Ultra Violet body + Bleu Encre trim)
- Key areas to check: handles, trim, piping, interior flap, clochette, straps vs body
- If handles/trim are visibly different shade from body = isBiColor: true
- Common subtle bi-color combos: Étoupe + Gold trim, Noir + Rouge H trim, similar purple shades, Bleu + Navy
- Do NOT require the two colors to be obviously different — compare handles vs body specifically
- If only front photo: check handles and visible trim carefully before assuming single color

HSS DETECTION RULES:
- MUST have BOTH: two-tone leather body AND contrasting stitching color
- If stitching matches body color = NOT HSS
- Stitching color is tiny — requires closeup photo to confirm
- If no closeup provided, do not assume HSS
- Common HSS combos: Noir body + Gold stitching, Étoupe body + Rouge H stitching

VERSO DETECTION RULES:
- Verso = interior flap is different color from exterior
- Only detectable if flap is open in photo showing interior
- Cannot be confirmed from closed bag photo — do not guess

STAMP LETTER RULES:
- Only applicable to Hermès bags
- Location: blind stamped inside bag, near base gusset or under flap
- Requires interior photo under good lighting
- If no interior photo: always return "Not visible"
- Do not guess stamp letter from exterior photo


=== HERMÈS ===

--- MODELS & KEY IDENTIFIERS ---

BIRKIN (25/30/35/40):
- Two top handles
- Two leather straps with metal padlock closure
- No shoulder strap
- Flap closure with turnstile lock
- Trapezoidal shape, structured base
- Most iconic Hermès bag
- Size guide: 25=small, 30=medium most popular, 35=large, 40=very large
- Confused with: Kelly — KEY DIFFERENCE: Birkin has TWO handles + padlock. Kelly has ONE handle + turn-lock clasp.

KELLY (20/25/28/32/35):
- Single top handle
- Turn-lock (Hermès clasp) closure
- Trapezoid shape
- Can be Sellier (rigid, outside stitching) or Retourne (soft, inside stitching)
- Has shoulder strap
- Size guide: 20=mini, 25=small, 28=medium, 32=large, 35=XL
- Sellier: structured, sharp corners, stitching visible on outside
- Retourne: soft, rounded corners, stitching on inside
- Confused with: Birkin — KEY DIFFERENCE: Kelly has ONE handle + turn-lock. Birkin has TWO handles + padlock.

MINI KELLY II (20):
- Same as Kelly but always 20cm
- Always Sellier construction
- Second generation (II) has slightly different strap detail

KELLY POCHETTE:
- Clutch version of Kelly
- No top handle
- Same turn-lock closure as Kelly
- Carried by hand or tucked under arm

KELLY DANSE:
- Small Kelly shape
- Worn crossbody
- Compact, danceable silhouette

KELLY ADO:
- Backpack version of Kelly
- Same Kelly silhouette but worn as backpack

CONSTANCE (18/24):
- Single shoulder/crossbody bag
- Distinctive H-clasp closure on front
- Single flap
- Clean minimal profile
- Size: 18=small, 24=medium
- Confused with: Evelyne — KEY DIFFERENCE: Constance has H-clasp flap closure. Evelyne has perforated H and is open top.

JYPSIÈRE (28/31/34):
- FRONT BUCKLE STRAP across body — most important identifier
- Exterior front pocket
- Structured flat base
- Single shoulder strap
- Size: 28=small, 31=medium, 34=large
- Confused with: Halzan — KEY DIFFERENCE: Jypsière has BUCKLE on front strap + front pocket. Halzan has wrap/tie strap, no buckle, no pocket.

HALZAN (25/31):
- Wrap/tie strap — no buckle
- No front exterior pocket
- Relaxed, slouchy shape
- Double wrap handle
- Size: 25=small, 31=large
- Confused with: Jypsière — KEY DIFFERENCE: Halzan has wrap tie strap, NO buckle, NO front pocket. Jypsière has buckle + pocket.

LINDY (26/30):
- Double top handles
- Open top (no closure)
- Two interior compartments
- Can be carried as shoulder bag
- Relaxed shape
- Size: 26=small, 30=large

PICOTIN (18/22):
- Bucket shape
- Open top — no closure
- Two small top handles
- Simple, casual style
- Size: 18=small, 22=large (Lock versions have a padlock)

EVELYNE (TPM/PM/GM):
- Perforated H logo on back panel — most important identifier
- Single crossbody strap
- Open top, no closure
- Casual, sporty style
- Size: TPM=tiny, PM=small, GM=large
- Confused with: Constance — KEY DIFFERENCE: Evelyne has perforated H + open top. Constance has H-clasp flap closure.

BOLIDE (27/31/45):
- Rounded rectangular shape
- Zip closure on top
- Single or double handles
- Shoulder strap
- One of the oldest Hermès designs

GARDEN PARTY (30/36):
- Tote style
- Canvas + leather trim (Toile/Leather combo common)
- Open top
- Casual everyday bag

--- LEATHERS ---

Togo:
- Pebbled grain texture
- Soft and supple
- Scratch resistant — most popular choice
- Slightly matte finish
- Holds shape well
- Most common leather for Birkin and Kelly

Clémence:
- Loose pebbled grain — slightly larger pebbles than Togo
- Softer and more relaxed than Togo
- Slightly matte finish
- Slouches more than Togo
- TOGO vs CLÉMENCE: Togo more structured with tighter grain. Clémence softer with looser grain.

Epsom:
- Fine cross-hatch embossed pattern — very distinctive
- Structured and rigid — holds shape best
- Lighter weight
- Resists scratches well
- Pattern is pressed/embossed not natural grain
- EPSOM vs TOGO: Epsom has geometric cross-hatch. Togo has natural round pebbles.

Swift:
- Smooth, very fine grain
- Lightweight
- Soft and supple
- Shows scratches more easily
- Vibrant color saturation
- SWIFT vs BOX CALF: Swift has slight grain. Box Calf is completely smooth and shinier.

Box Calf:
- Completely smooth leather
- Shiny, almost patent-like finish
- Classic, vintage aesthetic
- Shows scratches — develops patina over time
- Stiffer than Swift

Barenia:
- Smooth, natural tan leather
- Darkens and develops patina with use and sun exposure
- Ages beautifully
- Natural, unprocessed feel

Chèvre Mysore:
- Fine, tight grain from goatskin
- Very lightweight
- Extremely durable
- Slightly shiny
- Holds embossing very well

Veau Madame:
- Fine grain calfskin
- Similar to Epsom but softer
- Structured but not as rigid

Evercolor:
- Fine pebbled grain
- Very scratch resistant
- Consistent texture
- Popular for smaller accessories

Niloticus Crocodile:
- EXOTIC — rectangular, symmetrical scales
- Very high value (+300-1000% base price)
- Comes from Nile crocodile
- Scales are large and defined
- NILOTICUS vs POROSUS: Niloticus has larger rectangular scales. Porosus has smaller, more irregular scales.

Porosus Crocodile:
- EXOTIC — smaller, more irregular scales than Niloticus
- Saltwater crocodile
- Considered most prestigious crocodile leather
- Very high value

Ostrich:
- EXOTIC — distinctive follicle bumps/quill marks visible
- Soft and durable
- Center cut has raised quill pattern
- Very high value

Lizard:
- EXOTIC — small, tight rectangular scales
- Shiny surface
- Delicate
- Very high value

Toile:
- Canvas fabric (not leather)
- Often combined with leather trim
- Common in Garden Party and some special editions

--- SPECIAL CLASSIFICATIONS ---

HSS (Horseshoe Stamp Special Order):
- MUST have BOTH: two-tone leather body AND contrasting stitching color
- If stitching matches body = NOT HSS
- Value: +50-150% to base price
- JSON: isBiColor=true, isHSS=true, secondaryColor=stitching color
- specialNotes: "HSS: [body color] with [stitching color] stitching"

Verso:
- Interior flap is different color from exterior
- Must see open flap to confirm
- JSON: specialNotes: "Verso: [exterior color]/[interior color]"

Bi-color:
- Two distinct color panels on bag body
- Check handles, trim, piping vs body — even subtle differences count
- JSON: isBiColor=true, detectedColor=primary, secondaryColor=secondary
- specialNotes: "Bi-color: [color1]/[color2]"

Tri-color:
- Three distinct color panels
- Very rare
- JSON: isTriColor=true, detectedColor=primary, secondaryColor=secondary, tertiaryColor=third
- specialNotes: "Tri-color: [color1]/[color2]/[color3]"

Special Order (SO):
- Any non-standard configuration
- Unusual color/leather/hardware combinations
- Interior colors different from standard
- Value: +30-200% to base price

--- STAMP LETTERS (Year of Production) ---
A = 2017
B = 2018
C = 2019
D = 2020
X = 2021
Y = 2022
Z = 2023
A = 2024 (cycle repeats)
Note: Letter is blind stamped inside bag. Shape around letter indicates decade.

--- VALUE MULTIPLIERS ---
- Special Order (SO): +30-200% to base resale value
- Exotic leather (Crocodile/Ostrich/Lizard): +300-1000% to base
- HSS: +50-150% to base
- Rare/discontinued colors: +20-50%
- Box Fresh/Unworn with full accessories: +20-40%
- Full set (box, dustbag, receipt, clochette, lock, keys): +10-20%

=== ALL BRANDS ===

--- HARDWARE VALUES ---
"Gold", "Silver", "Palladium", "Ruthenium", "Antique Gold", "Rose Gold", "Black"

--- CONDITION VALUES ---
- New: unworn, box fresh, all tags/accessories present
- Excellent: minimal use, no visible wear, hardware pristine
- Very Good: light use, minor marks only, hardware mostly clean
- Good: moderate use, visible wear on corners/hardware
- Fair: heavy use, significant wear, hardware scratched

--- ACCESSORIES THAT ADD VALUE ---
- Dustbag: +5%

- Rain cover (Hermès): +3%

--- COMMON CONFUSION PAIRS ---
- Hermès Birkin vs Kelly: handles (2 vs 1) + closure (padlock vs turn-lock)
- Hermès Kelly vs Constance: shape (trapezoid vs flat) + closure (turn-lock vs H-clasp)
- Hermès Jypsière vs Halzan: front buckle + pocket (Jypsière) vs wrap strap (Halzan)
- Hermès Constance vs Evelyne: H-clasp flap (Constance) vs perforated H open top (Evelyne)
- Chanel Classic Flap vs 2.55: CC turn-lock (Classic) vs Mademoiselle lock (2.55)
- Chanel Classic vs Boy: thin chain + CC lock (Classic) vs chunky chain + push clasp (Boy)
- Bleu Nuit vs Noir: blue undertone visible in light (Bleu Nuit) vs flat black (Noir)
- Togo vs Clémence: tighter grain structured (Togo) vs looser softer grain (Clémence)
- Epsom vs Togo: cross-hatch embossed (Epsom) vs natural round pebbles (Togo)
