=== HERMES ===

--- MODELS & KEY IDENTIFIERS ---

BIRKIN (25/30/35/40):
- Two top handles
- Two leather straps with metal padlock closure
- No shoulder strap
- Flap closure with turnstile lock
- Trapezoidal shape, structured base
- Most iconic Hermes bag
- Size guide: 25=small, 30=medium most popular, 35=large, 40=very large
- Confused with: Kelly — KEY DIFFERENCE: Birkin has TWO handles + padlock. Kelly has ONE handle + turn-lock clasp.
- Special editions: Birkin Shadow (embossed shadow effect), Birkin Faubourg (building facade design)

KELLY (20/25/28/32/35):
- Single top handle
- Turn-lock (Hermes clasp) closure
- Trapezoid shape
- Can be Sellier (rigid, outside stitching) or Retourne (soft, inside stitching)
- Has shoulder strap
- Size guide: 20=mini, 25=small, 28=medium, 32=large, 35=XL
- Sellier: structured, sharp corners, stitching visible on outside
- Retourne: soft, rounded corners, stitching on inside
- Confused with: Birkin — KEY DIFFERENCE: Kelly has ONE handle + turn-lock. Birkin has TWO handles + padlock.
- Special editions: Kelly Picnic (Osier wicker), Kelly Idole, Kelly Cut (flat clutch version)

MINI KELLY II (20):
- Same as Kelly but always 20cm
- Always Sellier construction
- Second generation (II) has slightly different strap detail
- Very high resale value due to rarity and size

KELLY POCHETTE:
- Clutch version of Kelly
- No top handle
- Same turn-lock closure as Kelly
- Carried by hand or tucked under arm
- Often comes in exotic leathers

KELLY DANSE:
- Small Kelly shape worn crossbody
- Has exterior back pocket
- Casual sporty version of Kelly

KELLY ADO:
- Backpack version of Kelly
- Same Kelly silhouette but worn as backpack
- Rare and highly collectible

CONSTANCE (18/24):
- Single shoulder/crossbody bag
- Distinctive H-clasp closure on front — most important identifier
- Single flap, clean minimal profile
- Size: 18=small, 24=medium
- Slim/Elan version: longer and narrower
- Confused with: Evelyne — KEY DIFFERENCE: Constance has H-clasp flap closure. Evelyne has perforated H and is open top.

JYPSIERE (28/31/34):
- FRONT BUCKLE STRAP across body — most important identifier
- Exterior front pocket
- Structured flat base, single shoulder strap
- Size: 28=small, 31=medium, 34=large
- Confused with: Halzan — KEY DIFFERENCE: Jypsiere has BUCKLE on front strap + front pocket. Halzan has wrap/tie strap, no buckle, no pocket.

HALZAN (25/31):
- Wrap/tie strap — no buckle
- No front exterior pocket
- Relaxed, slouchy shape, double wrap handle
- Size: 25=small, 31=large
- Confused with: Jypsiere — KEY DIFFERENCE: Halzan has wrap tie strap, NO buckle, NO front pocket. Jypsiere has buckle + pocket.

LINDY (26/30):
- Double top handles, open top (no closure)
- Two interior compartments, can be shoulder bag
- Relaxed shape. Size: 26=small, 30=large. Mini Lindy also exists (20cm)

PICOTIN (18/22):
- Bucket shape, open top, two small top handles
- Simple casual style. Size: 18=small, 22=large
- Lock versions have a padlock on handle

EVELYNE (TPM/PM/GM):
- Perforated H logo on back panel — most important identifier
- Single crossbody strap, open top, no closure, casual sporty style
- Size: TPM=tiny, PM=small, GM=large
- Confused with: Constance — KEY DIFFERENCE: Evelyne has perforated H + open top. Constance has H-clasp flap closure.

BOLIDE (27/31/45):
- Rounded rectangular shape with zip closure on top — distinctive
- Single or double handles, shoulder strap
- Doctor bag silhouette, one of oldest Hermes designs

GARDEN PARTY (30/36):
- Tote style, canvas + leather trim (Toile/Leather combo common)
- Open top, casual everyday bag. TPM version also exists.

HERBAG (31/39):
- Canvas body with leather trim
- Removable interior pouch, casual interchangeable style
- Often two-tone canvas + leather

ROULIS (18/23/29):
- Small structured flap bag with distinctive curved flap shape
- Ring closure hardware, shoulder/crossbody strap

VERROU (15/21):
- Very structured rectangular shape
- Bolt/verrou closure hardware, evening/formal bag, rare and collectible

--- LEATHERS ---

Togo: pebbled grain, soft, scratch resistant, most popular, matte finish, holds shape. Most common for Birkin and Kelly.
Clemence: loose pebbled grain (larger than Togo), softer and more relaxed, matte, slouches more. TOGO vs CLEMENCE: Togo more structured tighter grain. Clemence softer looser grain.
Epsom: fine cross-hatch embossed pattern (very distinctive), structured, rigid, holds shape best, lighter weight, scratch resistant. EPSOM vs TOGO: Epsom has geometric cross-hatch. Togo has natural round pebbles.
Swift: smooth very fine grain, lightweight, soft, shows scratches easily, vibrant color. SWIFT vs BOX CALF: Swift has slight grain. Box Calf completely smooth and shinier.
Box Calf: completely smooth, shiny almost patent-like, classic vintage aesthetic, shows scratches, develops patina, stiffer than Swift.
Barenia: smooth natural tan leather, darkens with use and sun exposure, ages beautifully, natural feel. Barenia Faubourg is updated more durable version.
Chevre Mysore: fine tight grain from goatskin, very lightweight, extremely durable, slightly shiny, holds embossing very well.
Chevre de Coromandel: goatskin with more pronounced grain than Mysore, very durable.
Veau Madame: fine grain calfskin, similar to Epsom but softer, structured but not as rigid.
Evercolor: fine pebbled grain, very scratch resistant, consistent texture, popular for Constance and accessories.
Hunter: smooth leather with natural finish, develops rich patina, similar to Barenia.
Niloticus Crocodile: EXOTIC, rectangular symmetrical scales, largest scales, Nile crocodile, very high value (+300-1000%). NILOTICUS vs POROSUS: Niloticus has larger rectangular scales.
Porosus Crocodile: EXOTIC, smaller more irregular scales than Niloticus, saltwater crocodile, most prestigious crocodile, highest value.
Ostrich: EXOTIC, distinctive follicle bumps/quill marks visible, soft and durable, center cut has raised quill pattern, very high value.
Lizard: EXOTIC, small tight rectangular scales, shiny surface, delicate, very high value.
Python: EXOTIC, overlapping scale pattern, distinctive diamond/oval scale shapes, matte or shiny.
Toile: canvas fabric (not leather), often combined with leather trim, H pattern canvas most iconic.

--- HARDWARE ---
- Gold Hardware (GHW): warm yellow gold tone
- Palladium Hardware (PHW): cool silver tone — most common
- Brushed Gold Hardware (BGHW): matte brushed gold finish
- Ruthenium Hardware (RHW): dark gunmetal/charcoal tone
- Permabrass Hardware (PBHW): aged warm brass tone
- Black Hardware (BHW): matte black — rare, special order only
- Rose Gold Hardware: warm pink gold tone — rare

--- SPECIAL CLASSIFICATIONS ---

HSS (Horseshoe Stamp Special Order):
- MUST have BOTH: two-tone leather body AND contrasting stitching color
- If stitching matches body = NOT HSS. Value: +50-150% to base price.
- Known combos: Noir/Gold stitching, Etoupe/Rouge H stitching, Bleu Nuit/Gold stitching, Craie/Noir stitching
- JSON: isBiColor=true, isHSS=true, secondaryColor=stitching color
- specialNotes: "HSS: [body color] with [stitching color] stitching"

Verso: Interior flap different color from exterior. Must see open flap to confirm.
Known combos: Noir/Rouge H, Etoupe/Bleu, Gold/Rouge H
JSON: specialNotes: "Verso: [exterior color]/[interior color]"

Bi-color: Two distinct color panels. Check handles/trim vs body. Even subtle differences count.
JSON: isBiColor=true, detectedColor=primary, secondaryColor=secondary
specialNotes: "Bi-color: [color1]/[color2]"

Tri-color: Three distinct color panels. Very rare.
JSON: isTriColor=true, detectedColor=primary, secondaryColor=secondary, tertiaryColor=third
specialNotes: "Tri-color: [color1]/[color2]/[color3]"

Special Order (SO): Any non-standard configuration, unusual color/leather/hardware, exotic on standard model. Value: +30-200%

Faubourg Special Edition: Features Hermes Faubourg building facade embossed or printed. Very rare, very high value.
Shadow Editions: Embossed shadow of bag silhouette on leather. Tonal subtle effect. Special Order classification.
