=== GIVENCHY ===

--- BRAND NOTE ---
"GIVENCHY PARIS" embossed on interior
4G logo = primary hardware identifier
Clean architectural aesthetic — identified by shape + hardware
Less flashy than other brands — hardware is key differentiator

--- MODELS ---
Antigona (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: STRUCTURED TRAPEZOID ← #1 identifier
  → wider at top, narrows toward flat base
  → very geometric, architectural
  → rigid, holds shape perfectly when empty
CLOSURE: Zip across top
HANDLES: Short top handle + removable shoulder strap
BASE: Flat structured bottom with metal feet
CONFUSED WITH nothing — trapezoid shape completely distinctive
SIZES: Mini=20cm | Small=28cm | Medium=35cm | Large=45cm

Antigona Soft:
SHAPE: Same trapezoid silhouette as Antigona
MATERIAL: Softer leather, slightly slouchy ← differentiator
  → less rigid than original Antigona
  → trapezoid shape but relaxed
NOTE: Softer version of same silhouette

4G (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Structured flap, clean rectangle
CLOSURE: 4G LOGO CLASP ← #1 identifier
  → four G's interlocked forming square pattern
  → very distinctive geometric hardware
STRAP: Chain strap
CONFUSED WITH nothing — 4G clasp immediately identifiable
SIZES: Mini=tiny | Small=20cm | Medium=25cm

Voyou (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft hobo, relaxed unstructured ← #1 identifier
  → slouchy, collapses when set down
CLOSURE: Minimal — magnetic or open
STRAP: Shoulder strap
MATERIAL: Smooth leather most common
NOTE: Very minimal hardware — identified by slouchy shape + Givenchy embossing
CONFUSED WITH YSL Niki: Voyou is cleaner/less distressed.
  Niki has deliberately wrinkled leather + zip.

Cut Out:
SHAPE: Structured with ARCHITECTURAL CUTOUT DETAILS ← #1 identifier
  → deliberate geometric cutouts in leather body
  → sculptural statement piece
NOTE: Cutout design completely unique — immediately identifiable

Shark Lock (Mini/Small):
SHAPE: Small structured bag
HARDWARE: LOCK-SHAPED HARDWARE ← #1 identifier
  → oversized padlock-shaped clasp on front
  → very bold statement hardware
STRAP: Chain or leather
NOTE: Padlock hardware dominates the design

--- LEATHERS (photo identification) ---
Smooth Calfskin: Polished even surface, slight sheen
  → most common Givenchy material
  → used on Antigona, Voyou

Grained Leather: Pebbled texture, small uniform bumps
  → more casual/durable look
  → slightly matte appearance

4G Canvas: Logo canvas with 4G pattern
  → fabric texture, structured
  → tan/brown or black colorway

--- HARDWARE ---
4G Clasp: Four interlocked G's in square formation
  → 4G bag exclusive
  → most recognizable Givenchy hardware

Shark Lock: Oversized padlock shape
  → Shark Lock bag exclusive

Zip hardware: Branded zipper pulls
  → Antigona signature

HARDWARE COLORS:
Gold: Most common
Silver: Available on most models
Black: Seasonal/limited

--- COLORS ---
Noir: Black ← most classic
Blanc: Pure white
Ivory: Warm off-white
Blush: Pale soft pink
Dusty Pink: Muted rose
Red: True vivid red
Burgundy: Deep wine red
Camel: Warm tan
Cognac: Deep tan-brown
Navy: Deep navy
Cobalt: Vivid bright blue
Forest: Deep rich green
Taupe: Warm grey-brown
Anthracite: Dark charcoal
Storm Grey: Cool dark grey
Lilac: Soft purple

COLOR CONFUSION:
Noir vs Anthracite: Anthracite shows grey in light. Noir flat.
Ivory vs Blanc: Ivory is warm/cream. Blanc is pure cool white.
Burgundy vs Red: Burgundy has clear purple-wine depth. Red is pure vivid.
Taupe vs Camel: Taupe is grey-brown. Camel is yellow-brown.
Forest vs Cobalt: Easy to distinguish — Forest green. Cobalt blue.
  → in very dark photos both can appear near-black
  → check for color tint in any available light

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Antigona shape: Should maintain trapezoid rigidly
  → if shape is collapsing = structure damage or very heavy use
  → check corners — first to wear on structured bags
Smooth leather: Scratches show as lighter marks
Grained leather: More forgiving — minor marks less visible
4G clasp: Check clasp mechanism for scratching
  → gold plating wears on interlocked G's first
Shark Lock hardware: Large hardware = more surface to show wear
  → check plating on lock face
Zip (Antigona): Check zip slider for smooth operation
  → zipper pulls get heavy use — check integrity
4G Canvas: Check for fraying at edges
  → canvas can separate from leather trim
Base feet (Antigona): Check all four feet present
  → feet can loosen/fall off with heavy use
