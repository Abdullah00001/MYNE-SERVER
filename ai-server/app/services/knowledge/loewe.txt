=== LOEWE ===

Puzzle (Mini/Small/Medium/Large): GEOMETRIC PUZZLE-PIECE CONSTRUCTION (most important — multiple leather panels in different orientations), top handle + shoulder strap, structured architectural shape. Highly distinctive, very popular.

Hammock (Small/Medium/Large): Soft hammock-like shape, two short handles + longer strap, zip closure, relaxed.

Flamenco (Mini/Small/Medium): Soft drawstring bag, KNOT DETAIL at closure (distinctive), casual relaxed.

Gate (Small/Medium): Double gusset boxy shape, GATE/BAR CLOSURE hardware (distinctive), structured.

Amazona (19/28/36): Doctor bag shape, top handles, zip closure, classic Loewe design.

Balloon: Inflated/puffy round shape, architectural sculptural, modern.

Squeeze: Soft gathered/ruched detail (distinctive), evening statement bag.

LEATHERS: Nappa (extremely soft calfskin — Loewe signature, highest quality), Anagram Canvas (Loewe logo canvas), Suede, Raffia (woven natural fiber seasonal), Patent.
NOTE: Loewe is known for extremely high quality leather. Puzzle bag panels often show different textures deliberately.
HARDWARE: Gold/Brass (most common), Palladium/Silver
