=== VALENTINO ===

--- BRAND NOTE ---
Full name: VALENTINO GARAVANI embossed on interior
Studs are the most iconic Valentino identifier
V logo hardware appears on modern lines
Do not confuse with Valentino (the fashion brand) vs
Valentino Garavani (the bag/accessories brand) — same house

--- MODELS ---
Roman Stud (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured flap, clean rectangle
EXTERIOR: LARGE PYRAMID STUDS on quilted leather ← #1 identifier
  → oversized pyramid/spike studs arranged in grid pattern
  → studs are large, very prominent, 3D
QUILT: Diamond quilting UNDER the studs
CLOSURE: Flap, minimal clasp
STRAP: Chain strap
CONFUSED WITH Rockstud: Roman Stud has large studs ON quilted body.
  Rockstud has smaller spike studs ONLY on edges/trim.
SIZES: Small=20cm | Medium=24cm | Large=28cm

Rockstud (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Structured bag, clean lines
EXTERIOR: SPIKE/PYRAMID STUDS ONLY ON EDGES/TRIM ← #1 identifier
  → studs run along seams and edges only
  → body of bag is smooth, no studs on main surface
  → studs smaller than Roman Stud
CLOSURE: Various — zip or flap
STRAP: Top handle or shoulder strap
CONFUSED WITH Roman Stud: Rockstud = studs on edges only.
  Roman Stud = large studs covering entire quilted body.

Vring:
SHAPE: Small structured crossbody
CLOSURE: V LOGO RING HARDWARE ← #1 identifier
  → circular ring with V cutout forms the clasp
STRAP: Chain strap
NOTE: Very distinctive circular V hardware immediately identifiable

VLogo Signature (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Clean structured flap, modern minimal
CLOSURE: VLOGO HARDWARE clasp ← #1 identifier
  → V shaped hardware forms closure
STRAP: Chain strap
NOTE: Cleaner/more minimal than Rockstud aesthetic

Loco (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured flap, slightly boxy
CLOSURE: VLogo clasp
STRAP: Chain or leather
NOTE: Modern clean design, less embellishment than stud bags

--- STUD IDENTIFICATION (critical) ---
Roman Stud pyramids:
  → LARGE size — very prominent in photos
  → cover entire bag surface in grid pattern
  → sit ON TOP of quilted leather
  → 3D shadow visible around each stud

Rockstud spikes:
  → SMALLER than Roman Stud
  → appear ONLY at edges/seams/trim
  → body of bag between studs is clean smooth leather
  → run in a line, not covering full surface

PHOTO TIP:
Roman Stud = studs everywhere, large, quilted background
Rockstud = studs only at borders/edges, smooth center

--- LEATHERS ---
Smooth Calfskin: Polished even surface
  → most common, used on VLogo/Loco

Nappa: Soft buttery calfskin
  → slightly more relaxed than smooth calfskin

Quilted Nappa: Soft nappa with quilting
  → Roman Stud specific

Toile Iconographe: Monogram canvas
  → V logo pattern on fabric
  → seasonal

--- HARDWARE ---
V Ring (Vring): Circular ring with V cutout
VLogo Clasp: V shaped hardware
Roman Studs: Large pyramid hardware covering body
Rockstud spikes: Small pyramid hardware on edges

HARDWARE COLORS:
Antique Gold: Most common ← signature Valentino
  → slightly darker/more antique than bright gold
Gold: Bright yellow gold
Silver: Cool silver
Black: Seasonal

--- COLORS ---
Nero: Black ← most classic
Bianco: Pure white
Rosso Valentino: SIGNATURE VIVID RED ← most iconic Valentino color
  → pure saturated red, unmistakably intense
  → brighter/more vivid than any other brand's red
Blush: Soft pink
Rose Quartz: Pale dusty pink
Nude: Warm beige-pink
Ivory: Warm off-white
Camel: Warm tan
Cognac: Deep tan-brown
Antique Rose: Muted dusty vintage rose
Navy: Deep navy
Cobalt: Vivid blue
Burgundy: Deep wine red
Forest: Deep green
Lavender: Soft purple
Rockstud Pastels: Mint, baby blue, lavender (seasonal)

COLOR CONFUSION:
Rosso Valentino vs other reds:
  → Valentino red is notably MORE saturated/vivid
  → almost fluorescent intensity compared to standard red
  → if red looks extremely vivid/pure = likely Valentino red
Blush vs Rose Quartz: Blush warmer/more pink. Rose Quartz cooler/dustier.
Nude vs Ivory: Nude has pink undertone. Ivory is yellow-cream.
Antique Rose vs Blush: Antique Rose more muted/vintage. Blush cleaner pink.

--- HARDWARE COLOR + BAG COLOR COMBINATIONS ---
Most common/valuable combinations:
Black + Antique Gold: Most classic Rockstud
Rosso + Antique Gold: Most iconic Valentino combination
Nude + Gold: Very popular
White + Silver: Clean modern

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Roman Studs: Check each stud individually
  → studs can loosen and fall off with heavy use
  → missing studs visible in photos as empty holes
  → stud plating wears to reveal base metal
Rockstud edges: Check spike studs for bending/damage
  → edge studs get caught on surfaces, can bend
  → plating wears on tips first
Quilted leather (Roman Stud): Check diamond pattern for flattening
Smooth leather: Scratches show as lighter marks
Hardware: Antique gold plating wears to reveal brass underneath
  → check high contact points (clasp, strap hardware)
Chain strap: Check for darkening/loss of plating
