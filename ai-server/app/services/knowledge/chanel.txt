=== CHANEL ===

--- MODELS ---
Classic Flap (Small/Medium/Jumbo/Maxi):
SHAPE: Structured rectangle, medium depth
QUILT: Small uniform diamonds
CLOSURE: CC turn-lock (two C's rotate) ← #1 identifier
STRAP: Thin chain with leather woven through
CONFUSED WITH 2.55: lock shape — CC vs rectangular bar
CONFUSED WITH Boy: chain thickness + clasp type
SIZES: Small=23cm | Medium=25cm | Jumbo=30cm | Maxi=33cm

2.55 Reissue (224/225/226/227/228):
SHAPE: Same rectangle as Classic
QUILT: Diamonds BUT leather looks distressed/aged/wrinkled even when new
CLOSURE: Rectangular Mademoiselle lock (flat bar, NO CC logo) ← #1 identifier
STRAP: All-metal chain, no leather weave
HARDWARE: Aged gold/ruthenium (darker, antique look — not shiny gold)
BACK: Exterior zip pocket on back
CONFUSED WITH Classic: rectangular lock vs CC logo

Boy (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Chunky rectangle, noticeably THICKER depth than Classic
QUILT: Diamond or chevron depending on season
CLOSURE: Push-lock clasp (rectangular button, no CC) ← #1 identifier
STRAP: Chunky interlocking chain (heavier/thicker than Classic)
CONFUSED WITH Classic: chunky chain + push clasp vs thin chain + CC lock
SIZES: Small=20cm | Medium=25cm | Large=30cm

Mini Rectangular:
SHAPE: Tiny Classic Flap, landscape orientation
CLOSURE: CC turn-lock
STRAP: Chain, wearable long or doubled short
NOTE: Noticeably tiny — phone + cards only

Mini Square:
SHAPE: Equal height and width (square proportions)
CLOSURE: CC turn-lock
NOTE: Less common than rectangular mini

22 Bag (Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Soft unstructured hobo, collapses when set down
CLOSURE: Drawstring at top ← unique among Chanel bags
HANDLE: Chain top handle
NOTE: Only Chanel bag with drawstring

19 Bag (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft relaxed flap, slightly slouchy
QUILT: OVERSIZED CC logo quilting (much larger than Classic diamonds)
CLOSURE: CC closure, softer feel than Classic
STRAP: Mixed chain + thick leather strap
NOTE: Large quilt pattern is key visual difference from Classic

Coco Handle (Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Structured boxy
HANDLES: Short rigid top handle + removable chain strap ← #1 identifier
CLOSURE: CC turn-lock or zip
NOTE: Only structured Chanel with rigid top handle

Gabrielle (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft hobo/shoulder hybrid
STRAP: Convertible chain system, multiple wear options
EXTERIOR: Chanel lettering visible on strap hardware
NOTE: Very versatile strap = key identifier

WOC (Wallet on Chain):
SHAPE: Flat, wallet-thin, tiny
SIZE: ~19cm W — unmistakably small
CLOSURE: CC turn-lock or zip
STRAP: Delicate chain only

--- LEATHERS (photo identification) ---
Caviar: Pebbled surface, small uniform round bumps, structured, slight sheen
  → most common, holds shape, bumps visible even in low-res photos

Lambskin: Perfectly smooth, buttery, slight luminous glow
  → scratches show as lighter lines, looks almost velvety

Goatskin: Similar to caviar but bumps slightly irregular/flatter, more matte
  → less uniform than caviar, slightly duller finish

Aged Calfskin (2.55 only): Smooth but intentionally distressed/wrinkled
  → looks deliberately worn even brand new

Patent: Mirror-like high gloss, strong visible reflections

Tweed: Woven fabric, visible threads, often multicolor

Nylon: Lightweight fabric, matte finish, smooth surface, often quilted and multicolor
  → used in classic quilted nylon flaps and backpacks

Canvas: Structured fabric, stiffer than jersey, often printed or monogram pattern

Satin: Smooth shiny fabric, evening bag material, reflective but softer than patent

Grosgrain: Ribbed fabric texture, horizontal lines visible, matte finish

Sequined: Covered in small sequins/beads, very reflective, evening style

Jersey/Velvet: Fabric texture, clearly not leather

--- HARDWARE (photo identification) ---
Gold: Warm yellow-gold, shiny — most classic, highest demand
Silver: Cool silver-tone
Ruthenium: Dark grey-black metal, edgy
Aged Gold: Warm gold but intentionally duller/antique — used on 2.55

CC Turn-lock: Two interlocked C's, rotate mechanism, centered on flap
Mademoiselle Lock: Flat rectangular horizontal bar, minimal, no logo
Boy Push-lock: Rectangular architectural button clasp, more industrial

Chain — Thin leather-woven (Classic/19/Coco): gold or silver links + leather strip inside
Chain — All-metal (2.55/WOC): heavier, no leather, slightly darker
Chain — Chunky interlocking (Boy): visibly thicker/heavier links

--- COLORS ---
Noir: Pure flat black — most classic, highest resale
Blanc: Pure white
Beige: Warm signature Chanel beige — iconic neutral
Beige Clair: Lighter/more cream than standard beige
Dark Beige: Deeper greige-beige
Caramel: Warm amber-tan
Burgundy/Wine: Deep red-purple
Rouge/Red: True vivid red
Navy: Deep blue
Forest/Dark Green: Deep rich green
Emerald: Vivid bright green
Pink: Warm medium pink
Blush/Light Pink: Pale soft pink
Lilac/Lavender: Soft purple (seasonal)
Champagne: Warm metallic gold
Silver Metallic: Cool metallic
Multicolor: Multiple colors like pink yeloow or blue
Seasonal Tweed: Describe dominant + secondary colors (e.g. cream tweed with black and gold threads)

COLOR CONFUSION:
Noir vs Navy: In light, navy shows blue tint. Noir shows nothing.
Beige vs Beige Clair: Clair is noticeably lighter/creamier
Burgundy vs Noir: Burgundy shows red-purple in light. Noir is flat.
Caramel vs Beige: Caramel has clear orange-brown warmth. Beige is more neutral.

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Corners: First to wear — leather lightens or peels
Hardware: Gold plating wears to brass/silver underneath over time
Chain: Darkening/patina on links = age/use
Lambskin: Any scratches show immediately as lighter marks
Caviar: Pebbles may flatten slightly with heavy use
Lining: Visible fraying or staining through interior photos
SERIAL: Pre-2021=hologram sticker. Post-2021=NFC chip embedded in lining.
