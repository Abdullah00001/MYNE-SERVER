=== BALENCIAGA ===

--- BRAND NOTE ---
"BALENCIAGA" lettering embossed on interior + often on exterior hardware
Most iconic bag = City (motorcycle bag)
Arena leather = signature distressed material
Hardware color significantly affects value on City bag

--- MODELS ---
City (Mini/XS/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft MOTORCYCLE BAG silhouette ← #1 identifier
  → slouchy, unstructured, collapses when empty
  → multiple exterior zip pockets on front
  → wide zip across top
EXTERIOR: Multiple EXTERIOR POCKETS with zips ← #2 identifier
  → front has zip pocket(s)
  → sides have additional pockets
TRIM: WHIPSTITCH on all edges ← #3 identifier
  → visible stitching around every edge/seam
  → contrasting or matching thread
TASSELS: Zip pulls have leather tassels
STRAP: Flat shoulder strap + optional crossbody
MATERIAL: Arena leather most iconic
CONFUSED WITH nothing — motorcycle shape + whipstitch + multiple pockets unique
SIZES: Mini=tiny | XS=25cm | Small=30cm | Medium=38cm | Large=40cm

Hourglass (XS/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: STRUCTURED HOURGLASS/CURVED shape ← #1 identifier
  → curves inward at middle, wider at top and bottom
  → very architectural, holds shape rigidly
CLOSURE: BB logo clasp on front ← #2 identifier
HANDLES: Short top handle
STRAP: Removable shoulder strap
CONFUSED WITH nothing — hourglass curve completely unique silhouette
SIZES: XS=19cm | Small=23cm | Medium=31cm

Le Cagole (XS/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft structured, BIKER/ROCK aesthetic
EXTERIOR: MULTIPLE EXTERNAL POCKETS + BUCKLE DETAILS ← #1 identifier
  → similar to City but more buckles, more hardware
  → buckle straps across front
STRAP: Chain strap
MATERIAL: Smooth or crinkled leather
CONFUSED WITH City: Le Cagole has MORE buckles + chain strap.
  City has whipstitch + flat strap + tassels.

Neo Classic (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured top handle, envelope flap
HANDLES: Short top handle
CLOSURE: BB hardware clasp ← #1 identifier
STRAP: Removable shoulder strap
NOTE: More classic/formal than City aesthetic

Tote (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Clean structured tote
CLOSURE: Open top or zip
HANDLES: Top handles + shoulder strap
LOGO: Balenciaga lettering on exterior ← #1 identifier
NOTE: Very minimal, lettering is primary identifier

--- ARENA LEATHER (City bag signature) ---
APPEARANCE: Distressed/crinkled texture ← completely unique
  → leather looks intentionally worn/crinkled
  → uneven surface, not smooth
  → slight waxy appearance
  → color appears slightly uneven/varied = normal
PHOTO TIP: Crinkled texture visible even in low res photos
CONFUSED WITH: Damage — Arena leather ALWAYS looks distressed
  → new City bag looks "worn" — this is correct/intentional

ARENA vs SMOOTH LEATHER (City):
Arena: Crinkled, distressed, waxy, uneven texture
Smooth: Flat polished surface, even color
Both used on City bag — Arena is more classic/desirable

--- HARDWARE ---
BB Clasp: BB interlocked letters forming clasp
  → Hourglass + Neo Classic
Whipstitch trim: Visible stitching on edges
  → City exclusive
Tassels: Leather tassels on zip pulls
  → City signature detail
Buckles: Multiple buckle hardware
  → Le Cagole

HARDWARE COLORS (City bag — affects value significantly):
Classic Silver/Nickel: Most common, most classic
Gold: Less common, warmer look
Rose Gold: Sought after
Aged Silver: Darker silver, vintage look
Black/Ruthenium: Edgy, less common
IMPORTANT: Hardware color on City = significant value/authenticity point

ARENA LEATHER COLOR NOTE:
Arena leather shows color with slight variation/unevenness
  → same color bag looks slightly patchy = normal for Arena
  → not damage or fading
  → part of intentional distressed aesthetic

COLOR CONFUSION:
Vieux Rose vs Rose: Vieux Rose is muted/dusty vintage pink.
  Rose is cleaner/brighter pink.
Anthracite vs Noir: Anthracite shows grey in light. Noir flat black.
Olive vs Vert: Olive is muted/yellow-green. Vert is cleaner green.
Gris vs Anthracite: Gris is medium grey. Anthracite is very dark charcoal.

Blanc + Silver: Clean classic

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Arena leather: Pre-distressed = hard to assess wear
  → check color fading at handle attachment points
  → check for tears/cuts vs intentional distressing
  → genuine wear shows as color lightening on corners/base
Whipstitch (City): Check stitching for loosening/unraveling
  → stitching at corners most vulnerable
Tassels (City): Check for fraying at tassel tips
  → tassels wear quickly with daily use
Hourglass shape: Should maintain curve when empty
  → if hourglass loses shape = heavy use/structure damage
Hardware: Check plating on BB clasp, buckles
City zippers: Multiple zippers = multiple wear points
  → check all zipper pulls intact and functional
Base: City base gets heavy wear from setting bag down
  → check base corners for wear/scuffing
