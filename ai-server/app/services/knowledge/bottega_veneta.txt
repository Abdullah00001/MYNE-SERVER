=== BOTTEGA VENETA ===

--- WEAVE PATTERNS (identify first) ---
Intrecciato Standard: Tight weave, strips ~1cm wide, uniform pattern
  → classic BV, used across all models
Maxi Intrecciato: Same weave but strips ~3-4cm wide, very bold/chunky
  → introduced ~2019 Daniel Lee era, commands premium resale
NOTE: BV has NO visible logo anywhere — weave quality IS the identifier

--- MODELS ---
Jodie (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft hobo, slouchy, collapses when set down
WEAVE: Intrecciato throughout entire bag
CLOSURE: KNOT DETAIL at top center ← #1 identifier
  → leather twisted and knotted, no metal clasp
STRAP: Single shoulder strap, also woven
CONFUSED WITH Pouch: Jodie has knot closure + strap. Pouch has no handles at all.
CONFUSED WITH Arco: Jodie is soft/slouchy. Arco is structured with top handles.
SIZES: Mini=tiny | Small=everyday | Medium=generous | Large=oversized

Arco (33/48):
SHAPE: Structured tote, clean lines, flat base
WEAVE: Intrecciato throughout
HANDLES: Double SHORT top handles, rigid ← #1 identifier
CLOSURE: Open top (no closure)
NOTE: Very clean minimal structured look
CONFUSED WITH Jodie: Arco has rigid top handles + structure. Jodie is soft + knot.
SIZES: 33=smaller | 48=larger tote

Cassette (Mini/Regular):
SHAPE: Small structured rectangular flap
WEAVE: Intrecciato BUT with PADDED/PUFFED squares within weave ← #1 identifier
  → weave has 3D puffy quality, not flat
CLOSURE: Flap with intrecciato flap
STRAP: Chain strap
CONFUSED WITH standard Intrecciato: Cassette has puffed/padded dimension to weave

Pouch (Mini/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft pillow-like clutch, no handles
WEAVE: Intrecciato throughout
CLOSURE: None — open top, held closed by pillow shape
CARRY: Clutch only (no strap, no handles) ← #1 identifier
CONFUSED WITH Jodie: Pouch has NO handles/strap. Jodie has knot + strap.

Andiamo (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured tote with defined base
WEAVE: Intrecciato throughout
HANDLES: Top handles + optional strap
CLOSURE: Zip top ← differentiates from Arco (open top)
NOTE: More structured/formal than Arco

Sardine:
SHAPE: Small flat rectangular clutch
WEAVE: Intrecciato throughout
PROFILE: Very flat, minimal depth ← #1 identifier
CARRY: Clutch only

Knot:
SHAPE: Small evening clutch
WEAVE: Intrecciato throughout
CLOSURE: TWIST/KNOT metal closure ← #1 identifier
  → architectural twisted metal clasp, no flap
CARRY: Clutch only
NOTE: Evening bag, very small

--- WEAVE IDENTIFICATION FROM PHOTOS ---
Standard Intrecciato:
  → strips ~1cm, tight uniform grid pattern
  → flat appearance, structured surface
  → classic look

Maxi Intrecciato:
  → strips ~3-4cm, bold chunky squares
  → more dramatic shadow/texture in photos
  → visually very different — cannot confuse once seen

Padded Cassette weave:
  → standard width strips but each intersection is PUFFED/raised
  → 3D bubble quality visible in photos

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Weave: Check for pulled/loose strips — individual strips can loosen with use
Knot (Jodie): Check knot integrity — should be tight, not unraveling
Corners: Weave at corners wears first — strips may fray/loosen
Color: BV leather can show wear as lighter patches on high-contact areas
Maxi weave: Wider strips show scratches more visibly than standard
Interior: Suede lining — check for staining visible through open photos
Shape: Jodie/Pouch should look full and pillow-like when stuffed — 
  collapsed flat in photos may indicate very worn leather

=== PRADA ===

--- MATERIALS (identify first) ---
Saffiano: Cross-hatch embossed pattern, waxy surface, very structured
  → geometric diagonal grid clearly visible
  → most iconic Prada material, scratch resistant
  → holds shape rigidly, corners stay sharp
  → PHOTO TIP: Grid pattern visible even in low-res

Nappa: Smooth soft calfskin, buttery appearance
  → used on Cleo, Padded Nappa
  → softer/more relaxed look than Saffiano

Tessuto/Nylon (original): Lightweight nylon fabric
  → matte fabric texture, not leather
  → vintage Prada aesthetic

Re-Nylon (recycled): Same nylon look but recycled material
  → identical appearance to original nylon
  → used on Re-Edition bags

Patent: High-gloss mirror finish
  → strong visible reflections

COLOR + MATERIAL INTERACTION:
Saffiano shows color slightly lighter/more matte due to cross-hatch texture
Nappa shows color deeper/richer due to smooth surface

COLOR CONFUSION:
Rosso vs Fuoco: Rosso pure red. Fuoco has orange pull.
Granato vs Cerise: Granato deeper/more purple. Cerise brighter/more red.

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Saffiano: Very durable — check corners for peeling of embossed layer
  → cross-hatch may wear flat on high contact areas
Nappa: Check for scratches — shows as lighter marks on smooth surface
Nylon: Check for discoloration, staining, pulls in fabric
Triangle plaque: Check enamel for chips — very visible in photos
Hardware: Zipper pulls wear first — check plating
Logo plate: Check for scratches on metal lettering
