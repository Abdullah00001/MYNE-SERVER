=== GUCCI ===

--- MATERIALS (identify first) ---
GG Supreme Canvas: Coated canvas, beige/tan with GG monogram pattern
  → durable, structured, most common Gucci material
  → warm beige-brown tones, GG logo clearly visible

GG Matelasse: Quilted leather with GG logo embossed into quilting
  → used on Marmont exclusively
  → chevron/diagonal quilt pattern with GG logo

Leather (smooth calfskin): Clean polished surface, no pattern
  → used on Jackie, Bamboo, Horsebit

Velvet: Fabric with nap, seasonal
  → rich color, soft texture visible in photos

Web Stripe: Green-red-green stripe on canvas
  → Ophidia identifier, always present

--- MODELS ---
Dionysus (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured rectangular flap
CLOSURE: TIGER/LION HEAD CLASP ← unmistakable #1 identifier
  → ornate animal head with ring through mouth
  → mythological Dionysus figure, very decorative
STRAP: Chain strap
MATERIAL: GG Supreme canvas or leather
CONFUSED WITH nothing — animal head clasp completely unique
SIZES: Mini=tiny | Small=compact | Medium=daily | Large=generous

Marmont (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Soft structured flap, slightly rounded
QUILT: GG LOGO QUILTING on flap ← #1 identifier
  → chevron pattern with GG logo embossed into leather
CLOSURE: Flap, no visible clasp (magnetic)
STRAP: Gold chain with red/pink leather lining inside chain ← #2 identifier
  → distinctive red leather woven through chain links
HARDWARE: Double G logo on front
CONFUSED WITH Chanel Classic: Marmont has GG quilt + red-lined chain.
  Classic has diamond quilt + CC turn-lock + leather-woven chain
SIZES: Mini=tiny | Small=22cm | Medium=26cm | Large=30cm

Bamboo 1947:
SHAPE: Structured top handle bag
HANDLES: BAMBOO handle (natural bamboo material, hinged joint) ← #1 identifier
  → bamboo is unmistakable — natural material, warm honey color
  → hinged version: bamboo bends at joint
  → fixed version: rigid bamboo arc
CLOSURE: Flap or zip depending on version
MATERIAL: Leather body, bamboo handle
CONFUSED WITH Diana: 1947 has full bamboo top handle. Diana has smaller bamboo accent.

Jackie 1961 (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Soft hobo, slouchy, crescent shape
CLOSURE: PISTON CLASP ← #1 identifier
  → small cylindrical metal push-button on strap
  → very minimal, understated hardware
STRAP: Single shoulder strap with piston clasp
MATERIAL: Smooth leather most common
CONFUSED WITH YSL Niki: Jackie has piston clasp + single strap.
  Niki has zip + chain strap + distressed leather
SIZES: Mini=19cm | Small=28cm | Medium=36cm

Horsebit 1955 (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured rectangular shoulder bag
HARDWARE: HORSEBIT on front ← #1 identifier
  → equestrian stirrup/bit shape, interlocking rings
  → gold metal, very recognizable
STRAP: Leather shoulder strap
MATERIAL: GG Supreme canvas or leather
CONFUSED WITH nothing — horsebit hardware completely unique
SIZES: Mini=tiny | Small=24cm | Medium=29cm | Large=34cm

Ophidia (Mini/Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured, various silhouettes (crossbody/tote/mini)
MATERIAL: GG Supreme canvas
STRIPE: GREEN-RED-GREEN web stripe on front ← #1 identifier
  → always present, always same colorway
HARDWARE: Aged gold
NOTE: Very vintage aesthetic, web stripe unmistakable
CONFUSED WITH nothing — web stripe on GG canvas is unique

Blondie:
SHAPE: Structured small bag
CLOSURE: ROUND INTERLOCKING GG CIRCLE ← #1 identifier
  → circular GG logo forms the clasp/hardware
STRAP: Chain strap
NOTE: Circle hardware makes it immediately identifiable

Diana (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured tote
HANDLES: Top handles with SMALL BAMBOO ACCENT detail ← #1 identifier
  → bamboo is smaller/accent only vs full handle on 1947
CLOSURE: Open top or zip
CONFUSED WITH Bamboo 1947: Diana has bamboo accent only.
  1947 has full bamboo handle as primary feature

--- HARDWARE ---
Tiger/Lion Head (Dionysus): Ornate animal clasp, gold tone
Horsebit (Horsebit 1955): Equestrian bit shape, gold most common
Piston Clasp (Jackie): Tiny cylindrical push-button, minimal
Round GG Circle (Blondie): Circular interlocking GG
Double G Logo: Flat GG hardware on Marmont front
Web Stripe: Not hardware but green-red-green fabric stripe

HARDWARE COLORS:
Gold: Most common across all models
Silver: Less common
Aged Gold: Vintage models (Ophidia, Horsebit 1955)
  → darker/more antique than shiny gold

COLOR CONFUSION:
Rosso vs Hibiscus: Rosso is pure vivid red. Hibiscus deeper/more pink-red.
Camel vs Nude: Camel has clear tan warmth. Nude has pink undertone.
Sage vs Emerald: Sage is muted/grey-green. Emerald is vivid/saturated.
Aged Gold hardware vs Gold hardware:
  Aged Gold = darker, slightly dull, antique look
  Gold = bright shiny yellow-gold

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
GG Canvas: Check corners for peeling/cracking of canvas coating
  → canvas can separate from backing at corners with heavy use
Marmont quilting: Check GG quilt pattern for flattening
  → quilting loses definition with heavy use
Bamboo handle: Check bamboo for cracks/chips
  → bamboo can crack with heavy use or drops
  → lacquer coating can chip, shows as lighter patches
Marmont chain: Red leather lining inside chain
  → red leather can fray/wear, visible in close photos
Jackie piston: Check piston mechanism for stiffness/damage
Hardware: Gold plating on all models wears at contact points
Web stripe (Ophidia): Check for fraying at stripe edges
