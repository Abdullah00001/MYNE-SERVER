=== LOUIS VUITTON ===

--- CANVAS/MATERIAL LINES (identify before model) ---
Monogram: Tan/brown LV logos + flowers on dark brown coated canvas
  → warm brown tones, LV initials clearly visible

Damier Ebene: Dark brown + medium brown checkerboard, no visible logo
  → chess-board pattern, warm browns, "Louis Vuitton Paris" inside squares

Damier Azur: Cream + light blue-grey checkerboard
  → same chess pattern but cool light palette, very summery look

Damier Graphite: Dark grey + medium grey checkerboard
  → same chess pattern, masculine, cool grey tones

Epi: Textured leather with subtle diagonal grain lines, NO canvas pattern
  → comes in solid colors, leather not canvas, very clean minimal look

Mahina: Soft leather with PERFORATED LV logo pattern punched through
  → holes form LV pattern, very soft slouchy bags only

Empreinte: Smooth leather with LV monogram EMBOSSED (pressed in)
  → subtle monogram, luxurious smooth surface, solid colors

Taurillon: Smooth calfskin, minimal/no branding visible
  → cleanest most minimal LV, used on Capucines mainly

Monogram Reverse: Inverted colorway — brown LV logos on tan background
  → same pattern as Monogram but colors flipped

--- VACHETTA PATINA GUIDE (photo identification) ---
New/unused: Pale cream, almost white
Light use (0-6 months): Pale honey, slight warmth
Medium use (6mo-2yr): Honey to caramel, clearly tan
Heavy use (2yr+): Deep caramel to dark brown, very tan
IMPORTANT: Patina is NOT damage — it is natural and desirable
Dark patina ≠ dirty. Uneven patina = normal (sun exposure varies)
Water spots on vachetta = darker spots, visible in photos

COLOR CONFUSION:
Grenade vs Carmin: Grenade deeper/more wine. Carmin brighter true red.
Cassis vs Grenade: Cassis has clear purple. Grenade is red-dominant.
Bleu Infini vs Indigo: Infini darker/near-navy. Indigo lighter/brighter.
Piment vs Mandarine: Piment red-dominant. Mandarine clear orange.
Pivoine vs Rose Ballerine: Pivoine more dusty/muted. Ballerine cleaner soft pink.

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Canvas: Check corners for peeling/cracking of canvas coating
Vachetta: Note patina level + any water spots or stains
Hardware: Gold tone — check for wear revealing brass underneath
Zipper: LV branded zipper pull — check if intact
Lining: Visible through open top photos — check for staining
Stitching: Should be even, golden-yellow thread on Monogram canvas
