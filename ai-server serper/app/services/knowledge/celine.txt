=== CELINE ===

--- IMPORTANT BRAND NOTE ---
Pre-2018 (Phoebe Philo era): "CÉLINE" with accent — highly collectible, premium resale
Post-2018 (Hedi Slimane era): "CELINE" no accent — current production
Same bag from Philo era commands significantly higher price than Slimane era

--- MODELS ---
Luggage (Nano/Mini/Micro/Medium):
SHAPE: Distinctive FACE-LIKE silhouette ← unmistakable #1 identifier
  → two WING HANDLES on sides + top handle form face shape
  → flat bottom, structured
CLOSURE: Zip across top
HANDLES: Two short wing handles on sides + one top handle
CONFUSED WITH nothing — face silhouette completely unique
SIZES: Nano=18cm (tiny) | Mini=26cm | Micro=30cm | Medium=40cm
NOTE: Most iconic Celine bag, Phoebe Philo era — Nano most popular size

Classic Box (Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Perfectly boxy rectangle, very structured, rigid
CLOSURE: Flap with TURN-LOCK ← #1 identifier
STRAP: Single thin shoulder strap
MATERIAL: Smooth box calfskin, high shine
NOTE: Very collectible Philo era piece
  → clean minimalist boxy silhouette unlike anything else
CONFUSED WITH nothing in Celine lineup — rigidly boxy + turn-lock unique

Triomphe (Mini/Small/Medium):
SHAPE: Structured flap, clean rectangle
MATERIAL: TRIOMPHE CANVAS (interlocked C logo pattern) ← #1 identifier
CLOSURE: Flap, minimal hardware
STRAP: Chain strap
NOTE: Canvas logo pattern is primary identifier
CONFUSED WITH Teen Triomphe: Teen is noticeably larger version

Teen Triomphe:
SHAPE: Same as Triomphe but larger proportions
MATERIAL: Triomphe canvas
NOTE: Oversized version of standard Triomphe

Cabas (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Open structured tote, clean lines
CLOSURE: Open top
HANDLES: Top handles + optional strap
MATERIAL: Smooth leather or canvas
NOTE: Very minimal, no distinctive hardware — identified by clean simplicity

AVA (Mini/Regular):
SHAPE: Small structured handbag, slight trapezoid
HANDLES: Short top handle ← primary carry
CLOSURE: Zip or flap
NOTE: Clean minimal look, understated

16 (Small/Medium/Large):
SHAPE: Structured flap bag, slightly boxy
HANDLES: Short top handle + strap ← dual carry
CLOSURE: Flap with turn-lock
NOTE: Named after 16 rue Vivienne, clean architectural look
CONFUSED WITH Classic Box: 16 has top handle + strap. Classic Box has shoulder strap only.

--- LEATHERS (photo identification) ---
Smooth Calfskin: Polished, even surface, moderate sheen
  → most common Celine material
  → used on Luggage, Classic Box

Box Calfskin: Very smooth, HIGH SHINE, almost patent-like but softer
  → Classic Box signature material
  → PHOTO TIP: Notably shinier than standard smooth calfskin

Grained Calfskin: Pebbled surface, small uniform texture
  → more casual/durable look

Triomphe Canvas: Fabric with interlocked C logo pattern
  → used on Triomphe bags only
  → tan/brown colorway most common

Suede: Napped matte surface
  → seasonal

Patent: Mirror high gloss

--- HARDWARE ---
Turn-lock: Rotating rectangular lock
  → Classic Box + 16
  → very minimal, understated

Chain strap: Thin gold or silver chain
  → Triomphe

"CELINE PARIS" / "CÉLINE PARIS": Embossed on interior or exterior
  → accent = Philo era (pre-2018), premium
  → no accent = Slimane era (post-2018)

HARDWARE COLORS: Gold most common, Silver available

TRIOMPHE CANVAS COLORS:
Classic: Tan/brown logo on darker brown background
Black: Black logo on black background (subtle)
Seasonal: Various colorways — describe dominant color

COLOR CONFUSION:
Naturel vs Craie: Naturel is warm/yellow-cream. Craie is cooler/brighter.
Tan vs Camel: Tan is lighter/more neutral. Camel is warmer/more golden.
Cognac vs Camel: Cognac is deeper/darker. Camel is lighter/more golden.
Burgundy vs Brick: Burgundy has purple pull. Brick has orange-red warmth.
Anthracite vs Noir: Anthracite shows grey in light. Noir is flat black.

--- PHILO ERA IDENTIFICATION FROM PHOTOS ---
Pre-2018 bags show:
  → "CÉLINE" with accent on interior stamp
  → slightly different hardware finish (more matte gold)
  → Luggage in particular highly sought from this era
  → Classic Box from Philo era commands 2-3x premium
Post-2018 bags show:
  → "CELINE" no accent
  → more polished/shiny hardware
  → new models introduced (16, AVA etc)

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Box Calfskin: Shows scratches clearly due to high shine
  → scratches appear as dull marks against shiny surface
Smooth Calfskin: Minor scratches visible as lighter marks
Triomphe Canvas: Check for fraying at edges, staining on fabric
Corners: All structured bags wear at corners first
Turn-lock: Check for scratching/wear on rotating mechanism
Hardware: Gold plating wears on high-contact points
Luggage wing handles: Check stitching at handle attachment points
  → high stress area, stitching can loosen with heavy use
