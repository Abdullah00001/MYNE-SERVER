--- LEATHERS (photo identification) ---
Smooth Leather: Polished calfskin, even surface, slight sheen
  → most common YSL material

Grained Leather: Pebbled surface, small uniform texture
  → more durable, less formal than smooth

Matelasse: Quilted diagonal diamonds, structured surface
  → LOULOU EXCLUSIVE in YSL lineup

Vintage/Distressed Leather: Deliberately wrinkled, crinkled surface
  → NIKI EXCLUSIVE — intentional, not damage

Suede: Napped soft surface, matte

Patent: High gloss mirror surface

  → Niki front

Chain types:
Gold chain only (Loulou): thin delicate gold chain

COLOR CONFUSION:
Rouge Cabernet vs Bordeaux ← MOST IMPORTANT:
  Cabernet = purple-maroon undertone, very dark, almost plum in low light
  Bordeaux = warmer, browner, classic burgundy red
  TEST: In natural light — Cabernet shows purple. Bordeaux shows brown-red.
Rouge Eros vs Rouge Cabernet:
  Eros = bright vivid pure red, no depth
  Cabernet = very dark, deep, almost maroon
Noir vs Navy: Navy shows blue in light. Noir flat.
Craie vs Blanc Vintage: Craie cooler/brighter. Blanc Vintage warmer/creamier.
Nude vs Poudre: Nude is beige-dominant. Poudre has clear pink.
Fog vs Gris Souris: Fog has blue undertone. Souris is neutral grey.

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Smooth leather: Scratches show as lighter marks
Distressed leather (Niki): Pre-existing wrinkles make new wear harder to spot
  → check color fading on high-contact points instead
Matelasse (Loulou): Check diamond pattern for flattening
Hardware: Push-lock gold plating wears on clasp mechanism first
Chain: Gold chain darkens/loses shine with heavy use
Tassel (Kate): Check tassel for fraying at tip
Corners: Leather lightens at corners first on all models

=== CELINE ===

--- IMPORTANT BRAND NOTE ---
Pre-2018 (Phoebe Philo era): "CÉLINE" with accent — highly collectible, premium resale
Post-2018 (Hedi Slimane era): "CELINE" no accent — current production
Same bag from Philo era commands significantly higher price than Slimane era

--- MODELS ---

  → two WING HANDLES on sides + top handle form face shape
  → flat bottom, structured
CLOSURE: Zip across top
HANDLES: Two short wing handles on sides + one top handle
CONFUSED WITH nothing — face silhouette completely unique
SIZES: Nano=18cm (tiny) | Mini=26cm | Micro=30cm | Medium=40cm
NOTE: Most iconic Celine bag, Phoebe Philo era — Nano most popular size

  → "CELINE" no accent

--- CONDITION (photo indicators) ---
Box Calfskin: Shows scratches clearly due to high shine
  → scratches appear as dull marks against shiny surface
Smooth Calfskin: Minor scratches visible as lighter marks
Triomphe Canvas: Check for fraying at edges, staining on fabric
Corners: All structured bags wear at corners first
Turn-lock: Check for scratching/wear on rotating mechanism

Luggage wing handles: Check stitching at handle attachment points
  → high stress area, stitching can loosen with heavy use
